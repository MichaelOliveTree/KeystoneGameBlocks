Saying consciousness is informative means that every conscious experience specifies a particular state out of many possible options, reducing uncertainty and carrying rich, detailed meaning for the observer.

 This core idea is a major axiom in scientific frameworks like the Integrated Information Theory of Consciousness.

Core Meanings in Science and Philosophy

Specificity: Your mind picks one specific experience (like seeing the color red) while ruling out countless other possibilities.

Differentiation: A conscious system can separate and discriminate among a huge variety of different signals.

Meaning Generation: Consciousness does not just copy data like a simple wire; it actively turns physical inputs into a unified, meaningful internal picture.

Consciousness is the state of being awake and having subjective sensory experience, whereas free will is the alleged capacity to independently choose our own actions. While consciousness provides the raw arena for mental life, free will asks whether the conscious "self" actually steers the physical body. Opinions are mixed_opinions on Philosophy StackExchange regarding how deeply the two concepts intertwine.

Core Definitions

Consciousness: The basic awareness of internal and external environments, thoughts, and feelings.

Free Will: The philosophical and psychological belief that a conscious agent can author decisions free from absolute causal constraints.

Scientific and Philosophical Views

The Illusion Argument: Neuroscientific experiments suggest the brain initiates actions (via readiness potentials) milliseconds or seconds before a person feels a conscious intention to move, implying free will may be a retroactive story told by consciousness.

The Dependency View: Many people intuitively consider consciousness a strict requirement for free will; if an action is entirely unconscious or compelled, true personal agency disappears.

Evolutionary Adaptation: Some cognitive scientists argue both traits evolved cooperatively—consciousness to simulate future scenarios and culture, and free will as the advanced decision-making layer built on top of that foresight.
