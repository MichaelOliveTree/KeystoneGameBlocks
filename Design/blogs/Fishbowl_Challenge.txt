THESIS:
-----------------------------------------------------
Games can be made much more interesting if NPCs (Non-Player Characters) behaved in more "interesting" ways.  Games with more interesting NPCs will DRAMATICALLY INCREASE the global audience of interactive digital entertainment.  "Interesting" can be measured or tested by determining
	a) how long the average USER(S) will [want to] watch or observe a single NPC's BEHAVIOR in a simulation, without interacting directly with that NPC in any way AND
	b) where that NPC is restricted to a SINGLE SCENE or ENVIORNMENT [the EXHIBIT] that is no greater than 10x10 meters or 100 sqare meters (~1076 square feet for the Yanks).  

MISSION:
-----------------------------------------------------
NPC Behaviors in modern games are so EMBARASSINGLY and ATTROCIOUSLY bad, that I feel the Games Industry needs an INTERVENTION.  It simply does not seem to care much about advancing the quality of NPCs within their simulations.  Have we really come that much farther than the days of "Creatures", "Daikatana", the dog in "Fable", nVidia's 2002 "Dawn" demo and "The Sims?"  AI capabilities in games seem like they are always the first to get cut/postponed.  The industry seems to CONSTANTLY shut down/scale back the implementation of enhanced AI for NPCs in upcoming games due to "resource and time constraints."  

We know that people will watch

24 hours a day, but they WILL NOT sit around and watch the rediculously low-quality human-immitations that are Non-Player Characters in modern video games as they shift side-to-side in their DREADED/TIRED/LAZY/WTF ITS 2026 ISN IT?/LAME/ARMS & LEGS AKIMBO 'IDLE' ANIMATION.

Submitters should endeavor to create a life-like(TM) NPC that exists in the proposed enclosure/habitat/terrarium/exibit.

- Anything goes, but for priming the grey-matter, let's look at what behaviors one can attempt to simulate?
	- Autotomic(and similar) functions (eg. breathing, eye-blinking, hiccups, sneazing, yawning)
	- Appropriate responses to stimulii (outside noises, arrival of newspaper, mail or parcel or food delivery, thunder, rain, lawn mower, YOU  NAME IT!)
        - Emotional Range - emotions of various kinds performed in appropriate situations (receiving an unexpected letter of invitation to a FUNERAL of a close friend)
	- Dialogue (answering a ringing phone and engaging in interesting coversations with daughter, salesman, robocaller, wrong number, etc)
	- Navigation taken to new heights (dealing with a stubbed toe, walking around during a sudden power outage, inadvertant fall and determining the severity of any injury responding appropriately
	- Daily Routine - ability to have a routine but still adjust to random events (can your EXHIBIT implementation be so amazing that people would watch it for 7 days straight?)

EXHIBITION-A (aka "Scene 1") 
-----------------------------------------------------
This FISHBOWL CHALLENGE can be as deep/involved/detailed/difficult as one dares to make it.  This is the whole point!

This challenge should be of a TECHNICAL nature and not of an artistic or aesthetic one.  We are NOT trying to dazzle the USER with visuals, we are trying to dazzle them with NPC BEHAVIOR.  With this in mind, the Exhibits should all be very similar so that the various submissions can be evaluated\compared fairly.

With this in mind, for "Exhibit A" I propose a house on the order of 10x10 meters similar to the one depicted in the film "Up" and inhabited by a single NPC character.



EVALUATING THE SUBMISSIONS
-----------------------------------------------------
 

Hardware + Software
- Memory Use
- CPU / GPU Performance
- Size of code / libraries
- Simplicity/Elegance of the code
- Simplicity/Elegance of the API
- Extensibility of the code and the NPC Behaviors
- Scaleability

Fidelity of Simulation
- Real-time descion-making and responses with NO scripted behaviors.
- Believability (a chat-bot like test) [Un]Predictability of the  NPC  (moving away from the dreaded ARCHETYPES (2D cardboard caricatures) because we can't create fully fledged characters very easily/cheaply)
- NPC Learning (Memorizing new information and ways of achieving goals)
- # Simultaneous Behaviors that an NPC can run
- Existance of Emergent Behaviors 
	- I believe that emergent behaviors are more likely to arrise from simple/elegant systems (or systems with simple \ elegant rules) that can be combined in various ways to create more complex behaviors.


3D Engine & Tools
-----------------------------------------------------
Any 3D Engine and Tools you like so long as the end product can run on Windows, Linux or Mac and without the end user having to pay for or purchase any licenses, subscriptions or services to run the resulting software.
