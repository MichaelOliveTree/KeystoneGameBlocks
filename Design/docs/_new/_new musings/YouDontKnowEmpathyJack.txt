You Don't Know Empathy, Jack!

Most people think they know the difference between sympathy and empathy, but I wonder if they truly do.

Sympathy is essentially, feeling sorry for someone.  Sympathy can be regarded as a product of empathy.  Sympathizing with someone's pain and suffering, who has lived a VERY SIMILAR life as your own, is much easier to do because it does not require the intellectual persuit of attaining understanding because you already understand that person from a NON-intellectual, shared/common, life experience(s), matter of fact way.

Empathy is attaining a significant degree of understanding of another person's emotional state, perspective, situation or circumstance through the intellectual process of imagining how they got there... imagining (at least in significant/relevant part(s)) their life experience to that point.

Once you understand someone, it does in fact become easier to then sympathize with that person.  There is a quote I remember, but the author's name eludes me... it goes "Given enough time, one could learn to love anyone."  
This aligns with Jesus' teaching of "love thy neighbor" and "love thy enemy."  It is stating, if you seek to understand someone, and if given enough time, you WILL understand that person and you will be able to sympathize with them.

So empathy and sympathy are in fact closely linked. 

But to be clear, EMPATHY IS A SKILL.  It is not an emotion.  It is in fact a very valuable skill.  A shrewed(ahem... soulless) business person might research the founders of a competing company to discover anything they can about their life experiences so that they can understand their fears, their ambitions, their motivations and source of "vision" in order to help them predict their future actions... amongst other things.  Understanding therefore does not have to lead to sympathy.  Or, it does not have to lead to "sharing" ("knowing") the feelings of another.

Indeed, some people (low empathy having people, pyscopaths, sociopaths, antisocial personality disorder types) in this world may find they've been nurtured (as in the "nature (biological) vs nurture (enviornment)" debate) in such a way during their own development, that they are not fully capable of sharing in the pain and suffering of others, but they can understand understand it intellectually, and use that for the exploitation of others instead.
