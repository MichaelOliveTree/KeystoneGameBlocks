why captain kirk is the greatest scfi captain of all time


Captain Kirk is the archetype for Trek captains in every spinoff since.  Kirk could not be labeled or pigeon holed into a box... he was a fighter and a lover but not a war monger or a pacifist, he was not impulsive and could summon wisdom when needed.

In a way Kirk represents this ideal American spirit.  He wasn't a religious person nor was he an atheist, he was a skeptic but ultimately open minded and potentially could be convinced if the right evidence was available.
