splitting effort between world physical complexity and interactivity, and visuals to being very lopsided towards the visuals at the expense of the world simulation.  I think finally we're starting to come back to that but alot of game engines still must handle these things in a retrofitted way because they still dont want to sacrifice visuals and sacrificing visuals is what it would take to achieve the rich interactivity users want in an elegant engine.
