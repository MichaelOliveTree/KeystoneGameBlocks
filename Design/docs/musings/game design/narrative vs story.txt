narrative vs story

i think a good concicse way to think of narrative is thta narrative is the series of messages being conveyed and story is the vehicle for narrative.

consider that 2 people can tell a version of the same story and give it a different narrative based on their own biases, ideologies, etc.  Two people can describe a police shooting and in one, the narrative is about police brutality and oppression and in the other it's about black people being criminals, deserving to be shot and killed, etc.
