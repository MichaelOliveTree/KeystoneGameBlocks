Narrative wise, Candy Crush Saga conjures up imagery of childhood and the carefree excesses that sometimes go with it. It reminds us of games like Candy Land and stories like Hansel & Gretel...

There's a parallel between the narrative that's being told subliminally by the art direction and the gameplay mechanics and goals of the game... and the goals of it's creators.
