On "episodic" game design.


To avoid a semantics debate, I will accept that you're talking about a specific type of episodic game design that features melodramatic story telling and point & click adventure/puzzle.  This way we can avoid the numerous avenues of discussion involving various genres of games released or designed in an episodic way.  The key distinction I think is the business model which permeates the script of the games you're talking about.  I would list the 3 keys in order of importances as business model, melodramatic story telling, adventure/puzzle.  Unabashedly putting the business model first I think helps focus (dictates?) the script writing.  The business model permeating the script is what distinguishes "Lost" and "Heroes" from other TV series.  Knowing this, individuals can better decide if this is the type of game they are interested in making.
