there's this scifi game that looks very much a sci fi rip off of Minecraft.  

The interesting thing though is that it seems in many ways to work better than a minecraft fantasy game because there's a lot more mystery and wonder to a scifi realm where you're in some kind of Tatooine with a mix of aliens and a wild wild west atmosphere.

That is a GURPS "First In" style we'd like to have in our land portion of our Canon Saga exploration of worlds.  

However, it needs to also be more Trek'ish... wild wild west never existed and any world like that would be a place nobody would want to go.  This is part of the alien racism we see... that these aliens are all warlike and we're the rational reasonable ones who are just defending ourselves all the time.  It's a very Jewish philosophy i think.  "The world hates us and we are just defending ourselves. NEVER AGAIN!"


The key is how the look of the world felt like a place you had to use all your wits and tools to survive.  That theme is fine.  We want that.  Fighting against lower intelligent alien equivalent of bears and cougars is fine... but not portraying intelligent species as warlike monsters.

Discovery and searching of ruins for artifacts is also cool and designing these ruins as puzzles where player has to find a way to get the knowledge they hold.
	- ALSO KEY, knowledge may simply be ancient wisdom and philosophies and books.  Maybe the user is provided an encrypted link to some web page where ancient philosphy discussions take place... like a historical log of a debate between a teacher and a student and in this scroll the user just reads it.  Is there a mystery there or is it just an artifact that has no specific bearing on the game but is just something the user will think about and enjoy as some meta experience?
