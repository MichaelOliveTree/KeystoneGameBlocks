Memory Experiment Idea

i had a dream this morning around 8:30am where i had just dialed a phone in my dream... at the very instant i pressed the dial button, i woke up to the sound of my real phone ringing.


So i wondered :

1) Was it just a coincindence in timing
2) did the dream I thought i had, actually a false memory that was generated AFTER I woke up but the memory was placed in a period where I felt it had just occurred prior to me waking up
3) did i not hear the first ring of the phone so that the dream came in a semi-lucid state where it incorporated stimuli from the outside (the first ring while i was still asleep) and then when i woke up to hear the phone, it was actually already the second ring.


seems to me experiments with random people could determine which of the 3 occurred.

I think #2 would be crazy cool to prove because it would change the way we think about memories... that they arent linear afterall... that it's possible for a false memory (at least in terms of dreams) to occur ... they are false in the sense that there's no way the memory really could have occurred at the time you thought it did... in fact the entire dream of making the phone call would have been false... never happened since you were already awake when it happened.

the reason you could prove #2 is if it always happened on the first ring, and only one ring was audible and yet everyone wakes up believing they dreampt while asleep that they dialed the phone prior to the phone ringing. 

This might change the way we think of things such as deja-vu where one explanation is that your brain processes things more quickly and generates the false sense of having been there before.
