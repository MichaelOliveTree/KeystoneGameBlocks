altruism - thinking about the Ted Talk i saw last week about altruism and seems to me that there are other things that can maybe give us insight into altruism and why it occurs...

i think it comes from a sense of humility on the one hand, a sense that we know that we ourselves are in many respects lucky/fortunate, that we depended on others and have been shaped and influenced by others, and to the extent that those who are less fortunate than us have problems, it's because of circumstances mostly.  

if they had different circumstances they would've turned out differently.

then there is the question of why do we care when some people lie?  why do we care that a person is lying and taking advantage of others?  i think this 'caring' is closely related to our motives for other more stereotypical actions of altruism such as feeding, clothing, the poor.

I think it comes from this notion that we are a community, that we want a healthy community, that we want to be empowered and to try and change things and caring is the first step in trying to change things.


why do we care when some corporation is creating crappy products and selling them?  i think it starts with again, recognizing that someone is operating outside of the values we ourselves like, and that by them acting "badly" it will eventually effect us.  So it's a reaction to save our own communities... or to strengthen and improve them.
