On Aint It Cool News some guy asked "Whats with all the superhero angst in movies these days?" meaning why are we so into this seemingly all of a suden starting in 1999/2000.  I think the reason is fairly simple and it goes back to something I heard about memorabilia collectors.  

Those types of collectors are usually start in their 20's-40's and they desire to collect items from comics, movies, cartoons, and other popular entertainment sources from their childhoods.  So that is why alot of 30 year olds still seek Robotek models and toys and comics etc.  

Now when you talk about superman and batman and spiderman and now star wars and star trek, you have to remember that new kids are being born all the time and they corporations like Marvel and Disney and Lucas Arts, etc, all need to constantly recycle their IP into new cartoons, and merchandise and books, films every 10-20 years to introduce the youngest generations to these fantasy universes.  

In doing so they offer more material for the older people who grew up on these fantasy shows, and will likely want to watch the new stuff, but also they now setup the new geenration who will not only eventually like the new stuff, but may want to research the old stuff as well.   

So if you want to keep making money off your IP, you gotta keep re-introducing it for the young, but with an added benefit of the parents of these youngs are also familiar and excited about these re-inventions of their childhood favorites.
