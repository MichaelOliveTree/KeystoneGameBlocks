march.14.2014 - do no harm also equals do no help

i think this famed creed is just horrible.  its almost legalese.  

i do understand that historically doctors performed all sorts of things that made people worse often in ways that were different from their actual illness.

so i can understand "do no harm" as perhaps one of many rules of thumb or commandments... but I think there is also a popular belief that not helping someone when you can is morally ok... for example if the person cannot pay, or if the person is someone they dont like personally for whatever reason (lifestyle, personality, ethnicity, religion, etc)

and i think this is very much part of the modern culture of liberalism... a sort of neo fascisim where the belief of the day is the belief in nothing.  i guess really fascism is dead in a way because there's really very little nationalism because we dont have much to be proud of anymore.  and being proud of america feels very empty these days

we cant even lead on important issues... the curtain has been pulled back and not only cant we cling to "do good" we cant even cling to "do no harm" as we harm as a matter of course
