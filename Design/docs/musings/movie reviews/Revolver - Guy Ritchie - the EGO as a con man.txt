watched Revolver ysterday dir by Guy Ritchie and it made some provocative claims but i dont think its correct.

in fact, i think it would have been a better movie if instead of presenting these concepts as some sort of "con" that some invisible "ego" was playing on you, that it was more about the truth of how an "ego" is shaped.

I would say that an EGO is equivalent to a PERSONALITY.

The film never made that point.  How could it?  How could it say that YOU are not your personality?  

It would have to go from the position that somehow your personality is a construct over the real you. Ok, then what is the real you?  There is no way to answer this question.

The more productive response is to say, yes you really are your personality/ego, however, you have the power to change that through various practice of disciplines and conditioning and training.

This is an idea we can explore in one of our sub-stories in our CANON universe if we consider Canon is our Trek where we can examine all sorts of ideas and concepts and philosophies and politics, etc.
