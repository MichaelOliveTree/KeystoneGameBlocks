i want to rewatch Serpico because now it looks a lot like a film about a man working in a fucked up system... having to deal with discrimination, dirty cops, stupid cops, fucked up justice, the blue brotherhood, etc.

It's interesting to me now how i see the film differently... and it's one of the reasons good movies are worth watching again after 10 years.  I believe it was probably around 10 years ago when I first saw it... probably longer.  I was still at Microsoft perhaps.

Perspectives change over time and with it, you see the film with new eyes.  "And you'll return to where you started and you shall know it for the first time..."  Quotes like that too, you know for the first time when you have that sense that you have gotten a little wiser... when you actually experience the meaning of that quote yourself.  Abstract knowledge is difficult to fully appreciate... experience solidifies the knowledge.
