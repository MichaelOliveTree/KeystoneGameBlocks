March.22.2105 - Sneakers

ultimately, this is an adventure story not too unlike Indiana Jones, D&D, and Goonies and such.  The great american adventure... complete with a party of heroes each endowed with their own special talents... only this one takes place today, in the realm of IT.  It has elements of an espionage thriller, but really... that's just a veneer for what is ultimately a pure adventure story.

I think the key to an adventure story is comedy.  Comedy keeps the dramatic tone from turning the film into a brooding drama.  The other element is heroes who take on challenges that really are too big for them, and they succeed anyway against all odds... almost suggesting that "good" always triumphs over "evil."
