RE: Blue Ruin, Out of the Furnace, Super

Over the past few years we've seen a few films about the "good guy" getting even with the degenerate criminals and/or inbred hillbillies.

All of them well shot with solid performances and sometimes a bit of humor.  But they portray the day to day existence of working class folks as being like uncivilized animals, living in hell, their lives worth nothing.  There are no good guys.  Just dogs.  Just bleakness, despair and hopelessness.

There's no real point to these stories.  They're not really so much about revenge as they are about degrading the value of average people.

There will be more films like this to come.
