Just finished Lord of War starring Nicholas Cage as a gunrunner.

Lots of interesting (and sad) commentary.  

- Evil prevails (Evil prevails because good men fail to act... well if they are good men then why don't they act?  Thus, Evil Prevails.)
- Only the strong survive.  
	- ideologies is weakness
	- trying to change what you cannot change is stupid and will destroy you.
	- dont fight against your own nature.  
	- Only stupid criminals get caught.


Its hard to hate Cage's character in this movie.  On the one hand, you think to yourself, "I would never do what he's doing" and so you try to make yourself better than he is.  On the other hand, 99% of don't do shit to actually change the reality of the world we live in to make what he does unnecessary and unprofitable.  

He on the other hand, sees the world as it truely is and takes advantage.  How is that wrong?  

His character regrets his Brother's death and the way his family has left him, but I think in the end he is still able to rationalize these events to himself in a very scary (because it seems so true) logical way.  His brother died because of his own fault.  His wife turns on him because of her own guilt.  And his parents well... they put their faith in religion and other absurd concepts (in his mind).

A very complex character with alot of resolve.  For his character, he deeply believed in the "ok"ness of what he was doing.  I can respect his character for not wavering on that point.  If he woudl stop, it would be because his wife wanted him too, and not because he actually felt morally compelled.
