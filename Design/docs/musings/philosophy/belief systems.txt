belief systems


what i have come to understand about my own view on the world, it is no different from a "belief system"

the fact that one system is closer to the truth that the other is irrelevant when we are talking about how the belief system shapes our view of the world and how we think and act.

Belief systems are the filters through which we see the world.

this is why the pen is mightier than the sword because it can shape belief systems.

this is why propoganda is so dangerous when it is allowed to occur on massive coordinates scales by governments, corporations, etc.

this is why the 1st amendment (free speech) is only free and only relevant when all have equal access to reach people.  If a few powerful folks have ability to shape the minds of the masses, then there is no freedom of speech... it becomes the priveledge of speech.

when i think about the creativity that i have, the ideas i have, it all comes from my belief system that i think is not unique but not popularly held.  because my belief system is so different, the views and opinions i espouse just turn out to seem very original because people havent heard them.  but there are others who can in fact say them much more eeffectivley than i can, however i think the arts is where the views are more successful especially when espoused to members within an art community (eg gamedev)
