The very things that make the existing situation so volatile are the same things that make it appealing to many.

So one can advise companies to demonstrate to the public that they really do care about games by not cranking out marginally better sequels with more explosions every 18 months and instead focus on new and innovative games and not ship them until everyone on the team agrees... but the system is designed to win through exploitation, through fear and intimidation, 


When smart people do not change something that is broken, it's often because of systemic problems that are either beyond their capacity, their influence or their willingness to change.  We're talking about values.


So in a sense, the broken system is one that exists between a state of not 100% broken but far from well functioning...  and the people who work with the broken system come to accept the system rather than rock the boat to try and change it because "caring" is not rewarded.  "Caring" too much might get you fired.  Or you might miss your bonus.  Or what if you can blame the problem on someone else.
