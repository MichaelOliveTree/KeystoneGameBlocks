caging capitalism.  caging liberalism


- i think in all endeavors, the most successful are the ones who realize that even though our capitalistic society tries to tell us/instill in us that people should be specialists and then where employers treat employees as one dimensional tools... the most successful are the ones that can cage that mentality and not let it corrupt the humanity of the work environment.   

the work environments that maintain their humanity, that recognize the destruction of unrestrained capitalism and that view their fellow employees as full human beings with complex lives and complex emotions, these will be the most successful and most vibrant.  

Humans need to be nurtured NOT just by their parents or their immediate families, but by their teachers, fellow students,  coworkers, bosses, spirtual guides, etc.  Specialization can go too far.

I think when you think of the failure of the baby boomers, it was this notion that you could proclaim that specialization was fine, that nurturing was wishy washy bullshit but then, you sit back and curse the change in society as this attitude of not being responsible nurtures of the people you work with, results in a slide into deeper liberalism that will now also affect your own live.  So even as a practical matter, your laisse faire attitude (a liberal attitude) increases the rate of expansion of liberalism.  But they deny this and instead lament how the world is turning to shit when they decided not to help lead the way to a more conservative society.  

Ideology is not virtue.  Saying that "personal freedom is above all" is not a virtuous statement or a virtuous goal.  It's mostly just nonsense because it is unreasoned statement of fact.  And you can expose people's lack of rationality by challenging them on these sorts of statements.
