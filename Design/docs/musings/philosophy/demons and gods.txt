been watching The.Rite with Anthony Hopkins and since for the past 4 or 5 years ive entertained the notion that the bible could be an allegory that could make a lot of sense with simple word replacmeent....


one such world replacmeent idea from watching this movie comes from the word Demon.... so i think that sounds like demean which sounds kinda like under serving.. some maybe a demon is an under served person...  a low life thus is a demon because he was underserved... 

in this sense demons are created by man, by mans own inequality.   A God of course is the higher up... priveledged folk, above the others,...  


so just thinking in terms of what these words really mean... a Demon is a person who should be feared because he was underserved by his family, his friends and his people and so turned against them. 

similar story seems to be about Lucifer as the so called prince of the demons... cast out by his own...
