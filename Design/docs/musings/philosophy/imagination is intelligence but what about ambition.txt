imagination IS intelligence.  if you can imagine your own future, you can make it happen.  destroying imagination is key to oppressing populations.

when Russel Wilson's father used to tell his son "Why not you?" he was stoking the fires of his sons imagination.  he was not simply asking a question that when you look at it logically you start to find the odds are incredibly against you.  the purpose of this philosophy isn't to say "you can beat the odds" the purpose is to stir the imagination.

as we grow older and our lives turn out not the way we expect, it becomes more important than ever to hold onto our imaginations... to believe in a better future... to see ourselves in that future.

when you can no longer see a future for yourself, you are dead. and the darker the future seems, the faster you'll race towards death


BUT WHAT IS THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN IMAGINATION AND AMBITION?  Ambition can be a loaded term.  An ambitious mathmetician is intelligent but his goals may not really look like "ambition" which brings to mine a drive towards power, fame and fortune.  But there are other types of ambition that are more about discovery and curiosity.

Through the course of driving taxi, all of the strippers we drive to the clubs, you think some of these women are so beautiful they could do well in other lines of work or even in terms of finding a successful husband, but they have little personal ambition... their lack of personal ambition makes them DEPENDANT on others.  Ambition it seems of all kinds has the common denominator of personal freedom and expression and not having to depend on others...   though those seeking power and fame and fortune will USE others, they dont view it as being dependant on them per se.
