on "ideas are a dime a dozen"

ok, let's examine what is being said here


1 - or is it more along the lines of, everyone has ideas but most ideas are stupid?  And that it takes talent to recognize the really good ideas or come up with really good ideas?
2 - ostensibly, everyone has ideas, but what they dont have is the where withall to make those dreams a reality?

i think the danger is to accept the 2nd view and it seems more and more common that people do think in this way first.

the 2nd view really elevates power over the actual ideas themselves.  And by minimizing the importance of taht good idea, it's elevating all ideas to the same level... and it is indirectly telling us that we should value the powerful, the monied, more than the people who come up with good ideas.

this is also why patent laws are so unfair.
