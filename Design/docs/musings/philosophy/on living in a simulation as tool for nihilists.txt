the idea that we are all living in a computer simulation

i think its a valid philosophical question to ponder, however, i feel that for many the promotion of this idea is a backdoor way to spread nihilism... that selfishness might as well be the only point.

that is one of the reasons why i hope we dont live in a simulation... because it destroys the magic of the universe that i think we all want to believe is there


Moreover, people who push nihilism we dont even know if they themselves are really nihilist... it only benefits their own philosophies that the majority of the population believe in nothing.
