The Formula - Everyone has their idea of a formula and it revolves not around what you want most, it revolves around who your biggest enemy is.  

It's a curious thing how people think.  They see their fortunes as being inevitalbe if only they could just neutralize a few hurdles.


For the antisocial super rich with dreams of total world domination, the the enemy is the so called common man.  This is of course something of a parodox.  To dominate the world, it must have people and the more people the greater the sense of achievement upon attaining the goal.  But to dominate, the people also represent a hurdle that must be avoided one way or another.

Seems to me a big part of that dream has been met.  When you can lie the people into a war, get young men to signup and die for that war, get relatively little protests, nobody gets impeached or imprisoned, and EVEN WHEN THE REAL TRUTH COMES OUT, there's no revolution.  That sounds like a forula has been nearly perfected to me.
