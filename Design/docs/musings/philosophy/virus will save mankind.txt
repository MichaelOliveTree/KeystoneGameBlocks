humanity saved by a virus\bug


we see this theme a lot.. most famously in "War of the Worlds" and most recently in Brad Pitt's last film "World War Z" 

In today's internet/communications age, virality takes on a whole new meaning. Memes spread and there are areas of research (Memetics) that study the spread and mutation of these viral like pieces of information.

Can some piece of information (novel, multimedia, song) spread like wildfire and save humanity in the blink of an eye as it did in War of the Worlds?

As we get closer to this end (if that's even true), then does the infrastructure we need to ensure that spread can happen unobstructed by those who would try to stop it... maybe enough would not try to stop it...


When we look at the super wealthy and realize they too are nothing more than a type of social network... we must assume that in the event of some catastrophy, they would want to care for the people around them too.  That's what we all do and it's how we hold onto the illusion of our humanity even when we shit all over the environment.  The direct connections, we cannot fool ourselves so easily.
