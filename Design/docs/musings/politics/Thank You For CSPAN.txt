Everyone is always saying "Thank you for C-SPAN."  Why?  Well obviously there is categorically no other channel in the entire United States where viewers can hear more words spoken directly from the party in question's mouths.  That is to say, there's no hearesay, there's no spin, there's no interpretation.  The actual "Thank you for C-SPAN" reflects how starved and desperate America is for unbiased, unspun, truely fair and balanced reporting.
