- abstract entertainment is easier to make
		
- abstract entertainment is more popular
	- star wars is abstract fantasy of good vs evil with a coming of age story tacked on.
	- star trek (not always, but often) gives nuance to contemporary issues in society.
	
- all games have some level of abstraction by nature.  we don't have Matrix, Total Recall, or Holodecks afterall.

	- war games abstract away all the pain and suffering.  It's basically just lazy, mindless escapism at best. At worst?
	
- children often express themselves in abstract ways? they draw and paint and color abstractly.  Do they learn better with abstract expression?
	- is it appropriate for adults to hold onto that abstract way of expressing themselves?

- game theory is fundamnetally abstract thinking and is devoid of nuance.

- politics is full of abstractions.  left vs right, liberals vs conservative, etc.
	- abstract thinking makes it impossible to relate to people with opposing views.  You are good and they are evil.  There's no nuance.

		- there's no "Why was there slavery?"
		- why did the founders incite rebellion against the Crown? (well maybe some talk about no taxation without representation)
		- why did they choose a Republic?
		- what other forms of government did they consider?

- all art has some abstraction.
	- xkcd has abstract human figures but otherwise very nuanced and insightful ideas and topics.

	- wolfram's new way of thinking did show hoever that an abstract system can result in lots of complexity. hmm.
