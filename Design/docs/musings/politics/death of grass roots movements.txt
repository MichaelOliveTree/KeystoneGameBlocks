The Faux Grass Roots Movement


I think it's a serious question....  can any movement that is publicized extensively in the corporate media even be considered a grass roots movement?  The revolution will not be televised right?   I think it's a fair question and a scary one at that.  If even the movements we are told are grass roots arent, then my god... imagine all the movements you dont hear about, that never get any real steam... are they all dead?  How many real grass roots movemetns have died over the last 30 years?  All of them?

Have all the grass roots movements we've been told about been top down orchestrated implementations afterall?
