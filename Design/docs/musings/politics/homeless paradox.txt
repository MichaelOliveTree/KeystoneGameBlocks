homeless paradox

"if we will be judged by the way we treat our homeless, we will all be shamed"

that was quote from The Wire ep 5 given by the mayor during his news conference about stepping up to find the alleged serial killer.

But its timely because i had been talking and thinking about this quite a bit lately especially in the context of "in-name-only Christians"

how can we have homelessness if there are so many Christians in the USA?

----

but moreover, i think it shows how souless, scared, isolated (and isolation leads to selfishness and looking out for #1) we all are.  How we really have no ethic, no moral code...


and when watching the Wire, especially season 5, it talks so much about how easily most men lose their convictions and fight for power rather than stand up and risk everything for the people.  They all play their respective games.

McNulty essentially refused to play the game.  Lestor too for hte most part. but the fact remains, most will
