Right vs Left insanity


Right is never going to concede any points

It's like running in the special olympics, even if you win you're still a retard.


The strategy for dealing with the Right should be to treat them like they are mentally ill AND that they are a 20% party.  They can believe in whatever they want to believe but if you give them 50% airtime and coverage by following all their stupid tactics,then you are playing their game.

The Republican strategy is to sow the seeds of discontent... they are not planning on broadening their platform as a top priority, but rather as a piecemeal and targeted for specific regions but not generally.  The bigger obama can be made to fail, the bigger they suspect their results will be in 2012.

A strategy for dealing with Republicans is to not deal with anyone who is going to go online and preach the party line bullshit.  If you cant engage in reasonable debate, they simply need to be pushed off the airwaves.  This would be an avenue for a new range of Republicans like Megan McCain who are reasonable people.  These are the types of people that should be allowed to thrive in the void left by the Republicans.


so right now these republicans are fighting to stay relevant, to not be marginalized and that means not changing.
