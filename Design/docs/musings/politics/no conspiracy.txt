just writing this now but it was a couple of days ago i had what felt like an epiphany regarding conspiracy theories... that they are largely bullshit.  It seemed to me that if there really was this evil in the world, you'd have seen Africa whiped out.

In line with that i think is the idea that people are inherently good... selfish to a large degree but with limits as far as directly hurting another.


I think there's also a lesson in the idea of hurting/killing any living creature vs how we treat people. Its impossible to help everyone. It's impossible to live your life trying to help everyone.  Imagine trying to live your life without killing other living creatures... walking outside, on your carpet, taking a bath, you're killing living things constantly just to survive.  Eating vegetables even.  But i think the difference is intent... when the intent is not for food or when the intent is aimed directly at killing them to gain something they own or possess, then thats the line.  

To knowingly benefit from that must be bad though... meh, 


maybe not so much an epiphany but the feeling of truth ihad was that if there really was a global conspiracy (new world order) they could've taken over many places and the fact that you cant gather people up to go on an ethnic killing spree is testament as well
