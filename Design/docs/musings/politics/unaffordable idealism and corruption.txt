

Corruption thrives when idealism is unaffordable 


When people are dependant on their job for things like health care, food and shelter then they are often put into a position where they will ignore ills because the cost of speaking out or standing up is too great.

Thus in our society, the lack of public health care becomes a part of that economic enslavement and will be a part of the rise of corruption throughout the private and public sector.
