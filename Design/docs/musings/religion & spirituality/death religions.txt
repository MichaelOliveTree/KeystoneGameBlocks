So what of the fear of death and its impact on religion and beliefs?  Modern montheistic religions are referred to as "Death" religions by some since so much of it relates to dying and the afterlife and salvation of the soul.  In other words, so much is based on fear of death and consequences of your belief/practice in that religion (or lack thereof)

How might an enlightened belief system view death and spirituality?  It might involve hope and faith but no more can necessarily be discerned about its nature.  Therefore the persuit of knowledge hopes to shed light on this directly or indirectly thru inference into our biology (e.g. how the brain works)
