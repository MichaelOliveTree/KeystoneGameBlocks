the godfather trilogy is powerful in large part because it uses the devices

a) all sons suffers sins of the father
b) chronology of seeing the end result of the father and then seeing how a "good" average guy turns bad.  When we talk about "breaking bad" that is what Don Corleon did.  

That is powerful narrative that we all relate to... how our lives end up and if only we could see our own futures that way....

because in a way we succeed or fail by the virtuous or unvirtuous decisions in our present and past.  these may be a few large decisions or a string of tiny ones..

c) there's also a great look about corruption of people here.   if corruption can exist easily, it will exist easily.  that means risks are low  both in terms of getting caught (especially combined with burden of prosecutors to prove) and interms of punishment.  

when i think of taxi business, i see that if corruption is required to get ahead, corruption becomes institutionalized in a sense.  if others are corrupt and you are not, then you will get squeezed out.   capitalism enslaves us to money and thus enslaves us to corruption.
