midsomer murders


the british crime drama i find very interesting because of how it essentially pretends that life in these village towns are basically deviant and shallow and petty and such... almost as if to say "don't envy these people, YOU living in your hustle and bustle have it better!  you ARE better!"

and its a complete lie.  for starters, these types of murders just dont happen.  if they do, its really from outsiders coming in who are poor and trying to commit some crime.  its not this internal nest of rats all gnawing at one another.
