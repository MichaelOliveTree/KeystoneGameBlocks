Dec.30.2014  - Star Trek vs The Fallen

Star Trek was great because the crew was full of courageous, heroic, unselfish, kind, virtuous people who weren't racists, bigots, idealogues, or any such thing.  The philosophy of the show was to always have them be the best... examples of what we should strive to be.  

The show never really let them fall... 

Contrast this with a show like "Firefly" where in the name of being "gritty" or "realistic" we have characters who are all... fallen.  

What is the message this sends compared to the message that Trek sent?  or Lone Ranger and the old original Batman & Robin series with West.  

--

similarly, we see this trend towards "the fallen" in comedy... Groundhog Day and Planes Trains & Automobiles get turned into There's Something About Merry and Hangover and other garbage.  

It's even more stark when you look at a comedy like "My Man Godfrey" and such.
