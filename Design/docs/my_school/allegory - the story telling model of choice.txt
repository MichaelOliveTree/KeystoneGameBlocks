allegory is the story telling model of choice for laying out the truth

but our education system doesnt equip us to understand how to unwravel allegories

even the allegory of the bible is not unwravelled by most


- LotR - the one ring, capitalism to rule religion/philosphy, government, morality

you can take any fundamental truth, and make an allegory of it.  This is potentially a secret ingrediant to great stories with broad appeal.

in a way, this makes the allegory great for dishonest manipulation.  it clouds the real truth in a way that is similar to what the king's jester does.  makes us laugh and travesty and in so doing rob people of their righteous anger.  makes them accept the condition.

it can also make us fear the path of righteousness by portraying the path in the allegorical tale as wrought with danger and loss and then downplaying the value of the victory... making it seem too costly for too little gain.  it thus, minimizes the gain as something we shouldnt want.

but the above idea is probably a bit too crazy. lol.  just in one of my moods looking for things that arent there perhaps.
