july.28.2012


a written story is re-written many times

a game is prototyped and tweaked many times as it's being programmed

today we have the technology to completely prototype and then finally film a movie before any other production is done.  Storyboarding should be completely replaced with using something like Valve Movie Maker.

I think an entire new school of filmmakers should just start becoming very adept at using these sorts of tools.

Then the actual shoot can focus on the performances and the technical things related to getting the lighting correct and the cameras to follow the action properly, etc.

but the actual direction of the camera, the story, the dialogue can all be done and tweaked ahead of time so that no re-shoots are every necessary.
