- start a new GameDev website that encourages professionals to post.  

Gamasutra was a great resource, but sadly it wasn't the same after it rebranded to gamedeveloper.com 

Gamasutra was invaluable as way for professionals (as opposed to the newbie flooded GameDev.net) to discuss the industry and game development trends and processes.

Game developers need a site like NASA Space Flight where actual knowledgable people can discuss things.

i think that gamasutra discussion of Zynga actually impacted that companies bottom line.  Gamasutra had real influence.

It was also callling out predatory game dev practices.  

It was calling out the dopamine driven "fun" culture of game development.

It was debating ideas such as "the cultural ghetto" that Heckler talked about. 

It was influential in calling-out the myth that independant game development was dead and that garage game dev was over.

It was influential in predicting the rise of back-catalog reintroduction of old games.

It called out institutional bad behavior like unfair wages, harrassment, union forming, crunch, etc.

It reminded people of the history of games.

It was a rare independant voice in the industry.
