THe people are poor examples of a group of people trained by the philosophies and practices of the federation.



Philosophy must be excercised by practice and that means the practice of things like meditation, reciting of poems and works, and various other group activities like a Church.  The Church is a gathering place for a common philosophy... and it strengthens that philosophy... but a bad Church can strengthen bad philosophies.

I think there's nothing inherently wrong with shaping minds, reinforcing principles.   It is a conservative notion taht everyone be free to choose, but as far as how to strengthen a philosophy that is a matter of practice/ritual and indisputable.  Without practice, our morality (or faith to our philosophies) is erroded.
