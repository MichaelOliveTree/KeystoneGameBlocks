i think ive mentioned it in notes elsewehere but i think a great tool in determining if a person is trustworthy and honest (particularly politicians) is to watch their hands when they speak.  If their hands are held palm up a lot while they talk, and if their hand movements go palm up with an outward motion from their center to the audience, that is a sign of honesty... whereas closed fists or pointing (particularly with closed palm and palms down) that is a dishonest tell.
