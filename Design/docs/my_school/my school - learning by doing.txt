while in the shower a few minutes ago and after having watched various robotic videos on youtube (bird ornithopters, robot dragonflies, robot snakes, etc)  i started thinking again about my dream of making a boarding school for really educating kids a proper way.

the first thing i thought to remind myself of is that learning needs to be practical... that is it should be in the context of practical hands on experiment for making things.  Pure academic learning seems completely stupid.  Classroom sit in your seat for hours and just listen and take notes and memorize is such a primitive method.  We need to advance and really spend money on it by having the ability to really have hands on learning through practical excercises and projects.  How can you say you care about kids by wanting to give them healthcare when the real problems are that kids arent being taught jack shit about anything really.  


the big dilemna is that there is so much to teach and learn and i wonder if its possible to have retention of learning so many subjects.  i think the key is practical hands on experimentation where learning is accomplished by doing and so its reinforced in the most natural way.

sitting and watching and note taking is retarded.  instead, discussions should occur.  for subjects like philosophy and politics and history, i think there it makes sense to have sit down discussions but the sizes of the classrooms need to be small so that again, there is a proper chance for real involvement and engagement.  large class sizes for shut up and listen and take notes is retarded.  there's no engagement and the only kids who succeed here are the ones who basically learn to supress their own inner energy and just learn to learn the way they want you to but even these students potentially are stifled and dont realize it simply because they are getting "A"s on exams.

another basic principle i think is that teachers need to be humble and they need to preface their instruction and guidance to students with the stated reality that they dont have all the answers and that even experts can be wrong and what you'll be learning is based on what we believe to be correct but that you need to be flexible and curious and questioning of things that dont seem to make sense to you.
