What a no good piece of filth.  

That immediately got me thinking about how the entire system is geared to not produce really free thinking people, people who can educate themselves, and go out and do what they want and create and be NON specializers... or at least, can be temporary specializers and then quickly adapt and i think the key to that is a new education system model where we teach by doing.  

Sitting in a goddamn classroom and being taught to remember things is fine for basic things like learning to read and write and basic maths but there comes a point i think where the next step should be to teach kids how to teach themselves and then to have hands on workshops for things like wood working, metal working, electronics, etc.  

I think my ultimate dream has been to make this a reality... to make it so that you dont have to go to college in this day and age.  The other key would then be for proper socialization to replace the lack of social development (which is completely fubar in so many ways) from not going to college, is to have physical education and arts much more formal and to break the stigmas associated wtih dance class, etc.
