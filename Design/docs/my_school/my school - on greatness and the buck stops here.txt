dec.24.2014 - on greatness

qualties to be wealthy but not great

how can you be great when you don't understand how great people see the world?

a great man doesnt make a mistake that results in pain and suffering or even death of many and say "I didn't know." A great man realizes that it was his job to know and so he owns the responsibility... he accepts his fault.

a great man doesn't insult and degrade others because it's legal and then expect for the others to not break the law in order to get revenge.  in other words, great men don't hide behind the laws that were made to protect the wealthy 

In "Unforgiven" when Eastwood's character shoots the innkeeper and Little Bill says "You just shot an unarmed man!" and Will answers "Well he shoulda armed himself if he's gonna decorate his store with my friend."

In Poirot, season 10 E1 "Mystery of the Blue Train" i think, a train murder episode, a daughter of a father who committed suicide tells Poirot that her father did it because he entered into an agreement to sell the company with condition all workers be kept, but immediatley after the deal, the new owner fired all the workers and ruined the lives of a thousand families.  He killed himself because he knew that he shared in the blame and could not hide behind the "I didn't know" excuse as so many people do today.  Instead he accepted that a man in his position, with his great responsibilities, should have excercized great power in ensuring that the workers would be saved or else to not sell the company to that man.
