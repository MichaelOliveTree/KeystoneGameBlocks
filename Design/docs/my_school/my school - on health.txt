Was thinking about the boarding school id eventually like to start.  Part of the goal would be to truly teach without conditioning or brainwashing people into believing dogma just because.  I thought about the definition of mental health... what is it and why has it been neglected in schools?  Is it the absence of clinical disorders?  I think the answer is ismple...  mental health is a state of being in control.  Being in control means you are not stressed.  You are not embarrassed.  You are not afraid.  It's a feeling of knowing who you are and your capabilities and being able to persue your own interests free of burdens like depression, fear, doubt, etc.
