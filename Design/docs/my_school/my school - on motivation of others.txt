dec.23.2104 - The Nature of "Motivation" of Others

people seem to think that motivating others means making inspiring speeches that gets people excited.

other people seem to think that truly motivating others is impossible... that what we regard as motivation is so fleeting as to be nearly worthless, that ultimately motivation is always internal and that you cannot bring out what isn't already in the person.

By that last definition's standard, people are born with motivation or they are not... but the reality is, motivation is about creating an environment of safety for expression, safety within a brotherhood, and complete acceptance _if_ you do your part for the family... if you put the family first.  

When you look at the Seahawks, that is the kind of atmosphere I see.  When you see players giving it there all without even thinking... you're looking at players who know what is expected of them and who have accepted that and now act second of every down according to the code that is their ticket to continued acceptance.

So in a broader society, when you have people who are not accepted, who aren't given this sense that all they have to do is the right thing and they will be treated not as a minority, but as anyone else in the majority... then the ability to get people acting the way society wants them to act... is impaired.
