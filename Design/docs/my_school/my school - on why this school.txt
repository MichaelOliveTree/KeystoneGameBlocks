why do we train and educate you?  is it because you are special?  no.  nobody is special.  we do so because it is the moral obligation of the older generation to give their all to the development of youths who depend on their elders until they are ready to venture forth on their own.

just as it is the moral obligation of parents to provide the best they can for their children and just as it will be your moral obligation to provide the best you can for your children and the youth in your communities.
