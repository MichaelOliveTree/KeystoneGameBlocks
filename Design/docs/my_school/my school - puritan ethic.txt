just watched an interview by Cenk Unger from young turks

How Drunkards, Whores & 'The Immoral' Shaped the Nation (w/ Thaddeus Russell) 

Thaddeus Russell seems to me to be very much a Libertarian and although i dont agree with him entirely on anything, there is some truth to all of it.  

The most interesting parts to his talk and i suspect his book, is the idea that strong Democracy requires strong Puritan ethic and that weak Democracy, Liberalism... is enabled by weak Puritan ethic.

He talks about how MLK Jr preached Puritanism and demanded not just for govt to grant blacks equal protection in the eyes of the law, but for blacks to adopt white puritanical norms if they wanted to be able to walk through the doors of power that had been opened to them in the law


I think much of Russell's views are just libertarian extreme positions coated in some kind of pro liberalism, but his argument strikes me as full of inherent contradictions... and a convenient re-engineering of cause and effect...  

- for instance, he talks about how the sex drive must be curbed if you want to succeed as a capitalist... 
	- well, he makes it sound like any sex or thoughts of sex must be eliminated... but i think what we're really talking about is creating an ethic where sex and sexual energy is given a place in ones life rather than becomes the definition of one's life... where dancing, listening to music and having a few drinks can have a place in ones life, but it must not define our lives and our motivations and our identities and our dreams and goals.

still... liberals do have to come to terms with the idea that black poverty is not going to be solved by politicians or corporations, its got to be solved by a change in ETHICS. We can acknowledge causes that have helped drive black communities into poverty and crime and dispair, but the solution will only come through an education that instills ETHICS... not knowledge about math or science... that the ETHIC must come first.

so when i think about "My School" i think one of the missing pieces to the overall philosophy of what the school's purpose is... it's to instill that ETHIC and to teach it's value.
