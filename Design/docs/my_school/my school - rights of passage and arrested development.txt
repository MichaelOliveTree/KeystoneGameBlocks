So Michael Jackson died 3 days ago and a lot of talk about his emotional and mental age being like an 8 year old.  That got me started thinking on how perhaps the key ingrediant is that if you grow up in a fantasy land life and everything is being given to you and you're told what's important, you're not developing on your own at your own pace and you're skipping all these little rights of passage that other kids go through on the path to becoming a full adult.

I think perhaps that is something critical our school should do... make sure that kids are completing a path to adulthood AND that they understand that this path is critical. In other words for it to work it must be overt and told that its part of their education and development.   Our school isnt just  a place you go to learn facts, schools are places that help you develop into an adult.

So part of the curriculum or events and monitoring of students should be to ensure that they are meeting certain milestones eventually... 

the real question is how do you get kids to develop emotionally when some might be shy or slower (late bloomers)?
