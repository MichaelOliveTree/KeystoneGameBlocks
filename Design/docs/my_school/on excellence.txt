Excellence...

ive thought before about how the things we create, our bodies that we build and train and practice and do actions with... all of these things are about manifesting our will.

people think about crap like telekenisis and physic powers, but in reality it's as if these things are already manifest in us in other ways... in just our senses and our ability to create and use things



The other aspect of this which is a parallel of sorts... is the notion that intelligence on this planet is all basically part of a single organism... our progression and change is all tied together.  In this sense our morality to one another is an extension of our morality to ourselves and that is why there is no distinction between them.... true enlightment however is going beyond the intellectual "knowing" and ascending to the path that leads all action to be in harmony with this reality.

The challenge i have is that i want to reconcile advancements in technology in the face of global economic disparity, knowledge, opportunity, etc.
